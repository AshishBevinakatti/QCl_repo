## Provisional logo

`public/assets/resort/logo/provisional-catalogue-page-11-color-logo.png` is a 2360x1120 transparent PNG cropped directly from the 3150x3150 embedded page image in `Golden_Hills_Catalogue_Logo_Palette (1).pdf`, p. 11. The catalogue's cream matte was decontaminated while recovering alpha; no tracing or vectorization was used.

`public/assets/resort/logo/provisional-catalogue-page-11-light-logo.png` is an aligned light-surface derivative. Dark and green pixels were recolored to the catalogue cream while supplied gold pixels were retained. Both files are provisional raster derivatives, not approved brand masters; request the client's final SVG or AI before publication.

## Folder structure

- `source-assets/resort/scene-masters/` (outside `public/`): native embedded images or lossless reconstructions of embedded strips.
- `scenes/web/`: website-ready JPEGs and WebP derivatives, never enlarged beyond the native master.
- `logo/`: provisional extracted raster logo and documented light-surface derivative only.
- `previews/`: labelled review contact sheet.
- `asset-manifest.json`: dimensions, hashes, source pages, extraction methods, intended uses, and approval flags.

## Explicit exclusions

- Rooms and Cottages p. 36: excluded because of the visible watermark-like mark.
- Reception p. 8: excluded because of conflicting plural signage.
- Mood boards and third-party reference photography: excluded.
